What is Geography?

Geography is the study of the Earth’s physical features, human societies, and the interactions between people and their environments. It helps us understand climates, cultures, resources, and global connections.

Quick Geography Facts

  • Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world at 8,848 meters.
  • The Sahara Desert is the largest hot desert, covering 9 million km².
  • The Amazon River is the second longest river in the world.
  • Australia is the only continent that is also a country.
  • Antarctica contains 70% of the world’s freshwater in its ice sheets.

Continents of the World

The Earth has seven continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, and Australia. Each continent has unique cultures, landscapes, and biodiversity.

Earth Science Insights

Earth science explores geology, meteorology, oceanography, and environmental studies. It helps us understand earthquakes, volcanoes, weather patterns, and climate change, ensuring we protect our planet for future generations.
