What is General Knowledge?

General knowledge is a broad understanding of the world around us. It covers topics like history, science, geography, literature, and culture. Having strong general knowledge helps in competitive exams, interviews, and everyday conversations.

Quick Facts

  • The Earth revolves around the Sun once every 365 days.
  • The Great Pyramid of Giza is the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
  • Albert Einstein developed the theory of relativity in the early 20th century.
  • The Amazon rainforest produces 20% of the world's oxygen.
  • India is the world's largest democracy.

History Highlights

History teaches us about the rise and fall of civilizations, the struggles for freedom, and the progress of humanity. From the invention of the wheel to the digital revolution, history is a record of human achievement and lessons learned.

Science Insights

Science explains the natural world through observation and experimentation. It has given us electricity, medicine, space exploration, and the internet. Understanding science helps us innovate and solve global challenges.
